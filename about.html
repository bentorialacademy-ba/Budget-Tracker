Why it exists

Clarity should not require a complicated system.

Money management often gets buried under spreadsheets, categories, and good intentions. Budget Tracker keeps the daily task focused: record the money coming in, record the money going out, and see what remains.

It is designed for quick check-ins and steady habits, so your budget can support your life instead of becoming another thing to maintain.
